## English gloss

| # | Asks | Why it blocks work |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening hours, closing day, afternoon break | Goes verbatim into `openingHoursSpecification` JSON-LD. Also the #1 reason a call is wasted. |
| 2 | Exact founding year | A specific year is checkable; "over 15 years" reads as rounded. |
| 3 | Repairs and battery changes | `تعمیر ساعت` is high-intent and low-competition. May become the best-converting page. |
| 4 | Who backs the warranty | Trust copy must name a mechanism and an actor, never an adjective. |
| 5 | **What نمایندگی رسمی actually means** | Highest priority. Overclaiming a trademark relationship is a real legal risk and the wrong one to take on a trust site. |
| 6 | Is there a certificate to photograph | A photo of a real document beats any amount of design. |
| 7 | Which brands the dealer status covers | Decides which brand pages carry the claim. |
| 8 | Confirm the brand list | Determines site routing. Blocks Phase 01. |
| 9 | Is OBAKU still stocked | In the brief, absent from Instagram. |
| 10 | Catalogue size | Under ~60 is one filtered page; 300 needs per-brand pages. Different architecture. |
| 11 | Couple sets (`ست`) | IranTimer gives this equal billing with men's and women's. Major gifting category. |
| 12 | Kids' watches | `دخترانه` / `پسرانه` are distinct browse categories in Iran. |
| 13 | What he most wants to sell | The site should merchandise his priorities, not an alphabetical dump. |
| 14 | Primary number | The site needs one obvious primary, not three equal options. |
| 15 | WhatsApp / Bale / Telegram | 37% of Iranian shops use a different number for WhatsApp. A landline cannot have it at all. |
| 16 | Email | For `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD. Optional. |
| 17 | **Who answers, and their name** | A named human converts better than an anonymous shop, and no competitor can copy it. |
| 18 | **How he gives directions on the phone** | Ekbatan is a maze of phases and numbered bazaarchehs. His own sentence beats anything we would write. |
| 19 | Parking | Removes a reason not to visit. |
| 20 | Higher-res photos than Instagram | Decides all of Phase 04. Instagram re-compresses. |
| 21 | Domain already registered | Decides Phase 08. |

## Two things to do yourself, not ask

1. **Register the shop on Balad** at `business.balad.ir`. Free, no code, and it is the
   single highest-value non-website task for local discovery - Google Business Profile does
   not support Iran at all, so Balad is the substitute. Do it now so indexing starts early.

2. **Take photos of the shopfront and of him behind the counter.** A real photo of a real
   shop is the strongest authenticity signal available and costs nothing. This is the asset
   most likely to be missing at build time.
